Emotional Intelligence Organizations

Dedicated EI networks and research bodies — highest alignment with the book's core subject matter.

1. Six Seconds — The Emotional Intelligence Network High Priority
6seconds.org

The world's largest EI nonprofit, founded 1997. Offices in 10 countries, agents in ~50. They already publish and distribute free EI ebooks (Practicing EQ, 12 Days of Wellbeing, Coaching E-book) via their ebook library.

How to pitchContact form at 6seconds.org/about/contact or call US +1 831 763-1800. Jenny Wiley (certification): jenny.wiley@6seconds.org
Accepts ebooks?They publish their own, but accept content contributions from their network. Becoming a contributor/partner is the path.
Est. reach200,000+ email subscribers, millions of annual site visitors. Global network of certified practitioners.
2. Consortium for Research on Emotional Intelligence in Organizations (CREIO)
eiconsortium.org

Academic consortium headquartered at Rutgers University, co-founded with support from the Fetzer Institute (1996). 11 core members + 113 additional members. Provides free research and reference materials on their site. Co-sponsored by Yale Center for Emotional Intelligence.

How to pitchContact through Rutgers Organizational Psychology Program. Email via site contact. Reference their research.
Accepts ebooks?Focused on academic research. A practitioner-oriented ebook would need academic framing or endorsement.
Est. reachNiche but influential: researchers, corporate EI trainers, university programs globally.

Category 7

Library Systems & Free E-Book Platforms

How to get a free e-book into public library catalogs and free digital reading platforms.

22. Draft2Digital → OverDrive/Libby High Priority
draft2digital.com

Free aggregator that distributes ebooks to 38,000+ libraries and schools in 70+ countries via OverDrive (Libby app). Also distributes to Apple Books, B&N, Kobo, Scribd, Hoopla, Baker & Taylor, and more. Acquired Smashwords. Zero upfront cost.

How to submitCreate free account at draft2digital.com. Upload formatted manuscript + cover. Check "Library Services" box. Set price to free. Provides free ISBN.
Accepts ebooks?Yes — any self-published ebook. Free listing, free formatting tools, free ISBN. They take 10% commission (zero on free books).
Est. reach38,000+ libraries/schools. 70+ countries. Millions of Libby app users. Massive cascade across retail + library channels.
23. Internet Archive / Open Library
openlibrary.org • archive.org

Open, editable library catalog building toward a web page for every book published. Community-driven. Free account required for uploads. Has a Trusted Book Provider program for integration.

How to submitCreate free archive.org account. Upload ebook files directly. For catalog integration, contact info@archive.org.
Accepts ebooks?Yes — direct upload for original content you own rights to. Community then adds metadata, covers, links editions.
Est. reachMillions of monthly visitors. Permanent archive. Indexed globally.
24. Project Gutenberg Self Publishing

Accepts ebooks?Yes — accepts self-published original works. Must control digital rights.
Est. reachMillions of annual downloads from the Gutenberg ecosystem. Strong SEO and academic credibility.

Execution

Recommended Distribution Strategy

Prioritized action plan for maximum reach with minimum effort.

Phase 1: Immediate — Direct Submit Platforms (Week 1)

These require zero approval. Upload the ebook and it is live.

  • Draft2Digital — single upload cascades to 38,000+ libraries, Apple Books, B&N, Kobo, Scribd, Hoopla, Smashwords Store. The single highest-leverage action.
  • Internet Archive / Open Library — permanent, indexed, academically credible.
  • Obooko, ManyBooks, Free-Ebooks.net — quick listings on dedicated free ebook platforms.
  • Project Gutenberg Self Publishing — HTML + plain text version needed.
  • Goodreads Author Program — claim page, list book, join EI Listopia lists.
  • OER Commons — if willing to use Creative Commons license.

Phase 2: Outreach — Resource Libraries (Weeks 2-4)

Email pitches to organizations that curate free resources. Template: "Free e-book on emotional patterns by Shen (emotionmentoring.com). 20 chapters, 12.8K words. Available for your resource library at no cost. Here's the download link."

  • CredibleMind (40M+ lives) — highest-leverage curation target. One listing cascades to 150+ partners.
  • Six Seconds — already distributes free EI ebooks. Most aligned organization.
  • NAMI — target both national (info@nami.org) and local chapters individually.
  • Mental Health America — resource archive listing.
  • The Mental Health Coalition (info@thementalhealthcoalition.org) — resource database.
  • HelpGuide.org — recommended resource within their EI articles.
  • Well Being Trust — uses CredibleMind, so getting into CM covers this.
  • PositivePsychology.com — recommended reading list inclusion.

Phase 3: Content Partnerships — Write to Feature (Weeks 4-8)

These require writing content (guest posts, essays, blog proposals) that features or references the ebook.

  • Greater Good Science Center — submit personal essay (they pay $300). Feature ebook insights. Email: greater@berkeley.edu.
  • Psychology Today — submit blog proposal via Submittable. Feature ebook within posts. Massive SEO authority.
  • Tiny Buddha — guest post + ebook mention. Community-driven, accepts submissions.
  • Mindful.org — contributor article on emotional awareness.
  • ICF — position as a coaching tool for their 60,000+ members.

Phase 4: Institutional — Long Game (Months 2-6)

These require relationship-building and institutional credibility.

  • CASEL — resource library listing. Needs educational framing.
  • Center for Creative Leadership — guest content or co-created resource.
  • SIYLI — complementary post-training resource for their alumni.
  • ADAA — recommended reading for their patient community.
  • MHFA — supplementary resource for 2.5M+ trained individuals.
  • CREIO / Yale YCEI — academic partnerships. Needs research backing.
  • Mind UK — UK market entry through their resource pages.
