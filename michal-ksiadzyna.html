01 · Who he is

A practitioner, first.

Michal built The Shen Method through years of direct, one-on-one mentoring — thousands of hours of sessions with people carrying real weight. The mentoring grew out of that room, session by session, result by result.

His focus is the mind itself: the emotional blockages life leaves behind, and their repair. In the mentoring, clients recover a mind that adapts — decisions sharpen, relationships steady, fear shrinks.

Most of the people he works with lead companies. The rest carry loads of similar size.
