02 / The approach

Start with what is happening.
Understand what keeps returning.

Emotion Mentoring is the private relationship. The Shen Method is the approach Michał brings to it.

I.

Bring the real situation.

A difficult decision, a conflict, a reaction you cannot think your way out of.

II.

Find the pattern.

Find the emotions and patterns behind it, down to the source.

III.

Lead with a steadier mind.

Bring what emerges into your decisions. Weekly sessions keep continuity.

The aim: more room to think, a steadier response, greater capacity to lead.

05 / Questions

Clarity before
commitment.

What happens in the first conversation?

You describe your situation, Michał asks questions, and you both see whether Emotion Mentoring fits. No obligation to continue.

Do I need to be in a crisis?

No. Recurring pressure, a difficult relationship or carrying more than you have room for is enough to begin.

Is Emotion Mentoring therapy or business advice?

Neither. Emotion Mentoring works on emotional patterns, perspective and your capacity to carry responsibility. It is not medical or psychiatric treatment.
