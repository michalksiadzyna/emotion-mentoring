BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Clarity before
commitment.

What happens in the first conversation?

You describe your situation, Michał asks questions, and you both see whether Emotion Mentoring fits. No obligation to continue.

Do I need to be in a crisis?

No. Recurring pressure, a difficult relationship or carrying more than you have room for is enough to begin.

Is this therapy or business advice?

Neither. Emotion Mentoring works on emotional patterns, perspective and your capacity to carry responsibility. It is not medical or psychiatric treatment.

How long is the engagement?

12 months, with one private session every week. Change that holds needs continuity.

What if a crisis hits between sessions?

You have access to emergency sessions. In an acute crisis, Michał makes time within hours.

Is everything confidential?

Yes. What you bring stays between you and Michał.
