The method

The Shen Method
Built in the room.

The Shen Method was built in the room: more than 11 years, thousands of one-on-one hours, refined on real minds carrying real weight. It is a precise way of finding emotional damage and repairing it at the source. Every session applies it the way it was made: one on one, with Michał.
