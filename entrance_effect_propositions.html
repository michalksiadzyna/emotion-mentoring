Entrance Effect — 10 Scenarios

Each scenario runs 1 to 1.5 seconds, plays on the first visit only, skips on any input, and resolves into the calm page. All are buildable in the current stack (GSAP, an animation library already on the page, plus canvas, the browser's drawing surface). Grounded in what award galleries and luxury sites run now: slow graceful motion as a mark of luxury, editorial upward wipes, mask reveals, and luminous accents on cinematic dark. Answer with a letter.

Option A
The Gold Thread
0.0s black screen; a single champagne filament of light draws itself across the width, slightly arched. 0.5s the filament flares at its center, one soft pulse. 0.8s the line splits the darkness, top half lifting, bottom half sinking, like a curtain parting along the thread. 1.4s the hero stands; the thread's last glow settles into the golden beam under the CTA.
One line of light deciding the whole page. Restrained, surgical.
Option B
Molten Pour
0.0s a thin sheet of liquid gold pours down from the top edge, coating the black in a moving metallic sheen. 0.6s the sheen catches one bright specular sweep, the same gradient the headlines carry. 0.9s the gold drains downward and off-screen. 1.4s what it leaves behind is the finished page; the hero's metal text keeps the sheen as its shimmer.
The page is cast in gold, then cooled. Heavy craft signal.
Option C
The Signet Press
0.0s a champagne diamond mark, the business-card blueprint mark, scales down onto the black center like a seal pressed into wax; soft impact glow on contact. 0.6s it holds, one heartbeat. 0.9s the seal dissolves into fine gold dust that drifts upward. 1.5s the dust clears, the hero is standing beneath it.
A private letter opened. The closed-circle metaphor made visible.
Option D
Curtain of Bars
0.0s the screen is five vertical black panels, each edged with a hairline of gold. 0.3s they lift upward in a stagger, left to right, each revealing its slice of the hero. 0.9s as each panel clears, the hero letters underneath rise in sync with it. 1.3s the last panel exits; the page is whole.
The editorial award-gallery classic, tailored in champagne. Quiet confidence.
Option E
Light Sweep Horizon
0.0s a horizontal gold hairline fades in across the dead center of black, the same rule the sections use. 0.4s it pulses once, brightening from center outward. 0.7s the darkness peels away from the line, upward and downward at once. 1.3s the hero text emerges exactly where the line was, as if born from it.
A horizon at first light. Calm, inevitable.
Option F
Ink to Gold
0.0s the current ink-dissolve, re-choreographed: holes open along one diagonal wave instead of randomly, each ringed with a stronger champagne edge-light. 0.8s the wave crosses the screen. 1.1s one final bloom opens at the hero's first line. 1.5s its glow hands over to the metal shimmer of the headline.
The existing signature, given direction and a finale instead of randomness.
Option G
Chandelier Dust
0.0s a burst of fine gold particles falls from top center, slow, like chandelier dust caught in light; the page is already faintly visible behind. 0.7s the particles drift toward the hero's baseline. 1.1s they settle into the headline and fuse with the metal text. 1.5s stillness; only the standard shimmer remains.
Champagne in the air. The one scenario where nothing ever blocks the content.
Option H
The Aperture
0.0s total black; a circular iris opens from the center, its rim carrying the rotating metal gradient. 0.6s through the widening circle, the hero is visible already assembling. 1.0s the iris accelerates past the edges of the screen. 1.4s the rim's last arc shrinks into the diamond divider mark and fades.
A lens opening on someone who was already there. Cinematic in the literal sense.
Option I
Dual Doors
0.0s two heavy black panels meet at a thin gold seam down the center. 0.4s a low glow escapes through the seam, widening it by a breath. 0.7s the doors slide apart, left and right, unhurried. 1.4s the room behind them is the hero; the seam's glow dies last.
A private club opening its doors to one visitor. Exclusivity as motion.
Option J
Three Words
0.0s center of black, serif small-caps: Clarity. fades in and out. 0.45s Leadership. 0.9s Protection. under each, a gold hairline grows a little longer. 1.3s hard cut to the hero; the last hairline becomes the living beam under the CTA.
The high-end agency ritual, carrying the site's three promises before the page speaks.
