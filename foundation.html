The Shen Foundation

Further than private practice.

A Polish foundation established by Michał Ksiądzyna to advance emotional awareness and make emotion mentoring accessible to those who need it most.

01 · Mission

Why the Foundation exists.

The Shen Foundation exists to take Emotion Mentoring further than private practice ever could. We advance the physical, mental, and spiritual development of individuals through structured mentoring, education, and direct support.

We give more people access to the mentoring that creates real, lasting transformation.

02 · Leadership

The board.

Founding Member & President of the Board

Creator of Emotion Mentoring. A practitioner with deep experience guiding individuals through lasting emotional transformation. Graduate of the Wrocław University of Economics and Business. Published researcher and author.
