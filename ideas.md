## Vision

Premium 1-on-1 emotion mentoring for high-performers — CEOs, executives, entrepreneurs. Structural emotional transformation through direct nervous system work using the proprietary Shen Method. The practitioner IS the method. The inability to scale is the safety mechanism and the moat.

## Core Proposition

- Free 45-minute assessment as entry point
- Deep emotional work, not therapy — mentoring that operates on the emotional system directly
- Published methodology (The Secret Book of Emotions) builds trust before first contact
- Bilingual service (English + Polish) targeting international and Polish clients
- Shen Foundation as the credibility and institutional layer

## Insights from Research

### Failure Analysis (10 companies, $1B+ destroyed)
- Every venture that tried to automate or scale human coaching failed
- Trust is the currency — Cerebral, Talkspace broke trust and faced existential consequences
- Corporate budget dependency killed BetterUp; sell directly to the decision-maker
- 82% of coaching businesses fail within 2 years (ICF-cited)
- Coaches charging $200+/session see higher satisfaction and lower churn
- The practitioner being irreplaceable IS the moat (vs. Lantern, Calm — commoditized content)
- Sole real risk: founder burnout (Pattern 7)

### Competitive Landscape
- Premium method creators (Breathman, Wim Hof, Tony Robbins) all use: book as trust builder, certification for scale, content marketing
- Emotion Mentoring differentiator: proprietary method + direct practitioner delivery + no certification dilution
- Pricing sweet spot for premium 1-on-1: $200–500/session
