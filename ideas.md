## PRODUCT CANON (user definition, 2026-07-04 — overrides all prior descriptions)

The product is the upgrade of the mind and its functioning — the working of the mind itself. Deeper than decisions, problems, relationships; deeper than algorithms. What the work does:
1. **Repairs** — during life the mind/brain accumulates errors, damage, and unhandled emotional blockages that restrict its ability to adapt. The service works on exactly that.
2. **Improves function** — the mind runs better as a machine.
3. **Restores adaptation** — the mind becomes able to adapt to what it could not adapt to before.
4. **Unlocks** — new potential; emotional blockages open.
5. **Upgrades patterns** — improved patterns, new ways to solve problems.

Result: a mind that is more healthy, more productive, thinks better; higher intelligence — especially emotional intelligence, also social — and better handling of relationships.

**The flywheel:** works in work and out of work, because they are interconnected. A healthy family life fuels healthy functioning and productivity; higher productivity fuels a better family life. Both directions matter and both are part of the product.

**Positioning note:** "mind under load" is only ONE aspect, not the product. Product definition first, description/communication second. Positioning remains open until the description work is done on top of this canon.

**Why the CEO wants it:** CEOs are overloaded with responsibility, too much decision-making, and too many problems they were never prepared to handle — in professional AND private life. With this work, the mind becomes capable of handling all of it.

**Entry call:** 20–30 minute conversation framing confirmed by user ("20 to 30 minutes" reads even lighter than 30). Never "assessment."

**Scope of problems handled (user, 2026-07-04):** conflict management, imposter syndrome, unpreparedness for the role, mind load and the damage it produces in experienced CEOs, trauma, fear — anything hindering performance, decision-making, and outcomes. Emotions are the working layer beneath all of it.

**Loneliness at the top — FLAGGED PRIORITY (user, 2026-07-04):** a very popular problem among CEOs, and the emotional work directly helps handle it. Feature it prominently in the problem section. Evidence already in hand: half of CEOs report loneliness, 61% say it hinders performance (RHR International via HBR); two-thirds receive no outside advice (Stanford GSB); the friendship recession data. Copy angle: the mentoring itself is the one truthful room — the place where the person at the top can take their real mind.

**Outcome language directive (user, 2026-07-04):** copy speaks in outcomes — they make better decisions; they handle problems better; their minds are less taxed because the mind works better; they are happier and more fulfilled in their work; they are less afraid. Every claim stays inside the palette of results clients actually reported (pending Q9 answer).

**Statistics rule:** every statistic used on the website requires the exact primary study, verified, with reference. **The arsenal lives in `evidence_arsenal.html` (2026-07-04)** — cleared claims with exact wording + attribution, a banned folklore list, and a deployment map. Key correction: never "68% of CEOs weren't prepared" — the verified claim is "only 32% felt FULLY prepared" (Egon Zehnder primary). Killed: "26% executives depressed vs 18%" (broken citation). "You are in the majority" social proof is a core selling mechanic — the visitor recognizes their hidden problem as statistically normal among peers, which dissolves shame and stigma-based resistance.

## Insights from Research

### Failure Analysis (10 companies, $1B+ destroyed)
- Every venture that tried to automate or scale human coaching failed
- Trust is the currency — Cerebral, Talkspace broke trust and faced existential consequences
- Corporate budget dependency killed BetterUp; sell directly to the decision-maker
- 82% of coaching businesses fail within 2 years (ICF-cited)
- Coaches charging $200+/session see higher satisfaction and lower churn
- The practitioner being irreplaceable IS the moat (vs. Lantern, Calm — commoditized content)
- Sole real risk: founder burnout (Pattern 7)

### Competitive Landscape
- Premium method creators (Breathman, Wim Hof, Tony Robbins) all use: book as trust builder, certification for scale, content marketing
- Emotion Mentoring differentiator: proprietary method + direct practitioner delivery + no certification dilution
- Pricing sweet spot for premium 1-on-1: $200–500/session
