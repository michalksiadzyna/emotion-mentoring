The Shen Foundation

Established by Michał Ksiądzyna to advance emotional awareness and carry Emotion Mentoring further than private practice ever could.

01Mission

Why the Foundation exists.

The Shen Foundation exists to take Emotion Mentoring further than private practice ever could. We advance the physical, mental, and spiritual development of individuals through structured mentoring, education, and direct support. We give more people access to Emotion Mentoring and the real, lasting transformation it creates.

02The Work

Three ways the mission moves.

✦

Mentoring

Structured one-on-one work that finds emotional blockages at their source and repairs them where they began.

✦

Education

Teaching how emotions are built, how they shape decisions, and how the mind adapts once the design beneath it changes.

✦

Direct Support

Opening the door to Emotion Mentoring for those who need it most.
