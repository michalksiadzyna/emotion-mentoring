THE APPROACH

The Shen Method.
Built in the room.

The Shen Method goes to the emotional sources of recurring reactions: what you carry, where it comes from, and how it shapes the way you meet your life.

ONE TO ONE WITH MICHAŁ

Experience
shaped by practice.

The Shen Method was built in the room: more than 11 years, thousands of one-on-one hours, refined on real minds carrying real weight. It is a precise way of finding emotional damage and repairing it at the source.

Emotion Mentoring is the private relationship in which he applies it. A session begins with what is happening in your life and focuses on the emotions and patterns involved.

The aim is to work with the source of a recurring reaction and bring greater perspective and capacity into your everyday decisions. Weekly sessions keep continuity.

WHAT IT IS FOR

The aims of Emotion Mentoring.

A stronger mind
under responsibility.

01More room to think.
Work with mental overload and the emotional pressures taking up your attention.
02A steadier response.
Explore recurring patterns in conflict and difficult relationships, with more space between the feeling and your response.
03Support when pressure rises.
Bring personal or professional difficulties into a consistent, one-on-one relationship.
04A place to speak freely.
Think through decisions and experiences that are difficult to discuss with employees, partners, family or friends.
05Greater capacity to lead.
Work on the mind behind your decisions and the responsibility you carry for others.
