Questions
01What does it cost?+
Engagements begin at €3,000 per month and run six months minimum. Two deep sessions each month, with direct access between them. Twelve engagements exist at once.
02How is this different from therapy or coaching?+
Therapy revisits the past. Coaching manages behavior. This work goes to the emotional system itself and changes the patterns at their source. The results are structural; they compound.
03How does the engagement run?+
Private and one on one, worldwide. Two sessions each month carry the deep work; direct access between sessions carries everything the weeks bring.
04Who is this for?+
Built for CEOs. The seat with the most riding on one mind. We also work with those who carry large wealth and the weight that arrives with it.
05Is it confidential?+
Completely. Client identities are never confirmed, and the work itself stays in the room. Most clients tell no one; that is normal here.
06How do We begin?+
One confidential conversation of 20 to 30 minutes. Both sides decide whether the fit is real. Nothing is sold on the call.
